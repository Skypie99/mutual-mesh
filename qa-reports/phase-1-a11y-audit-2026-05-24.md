## 3. TL;DR

Four screens, 11 WCAG criteria each = **44 cells**. Result: **34 ✅, 9 ⚠️, 1 ❌, 0 launch-blockers.**

Tokens are clean — no raw hex snuck into any of the 4 screens or the components they use; the only literal-color references are intentional and audited (`text-white` on saturated status pills + flash banner backgrounds, `bg-black/50` on the modal backdrop). The Cycle 1 baseline (header roles, 44pt targets, polite live regions on errors, OS-driven reduce-motion) is intact in all 4 new screens.

The biggest gap is **focus management on modals** (claim + delete account): `ConfirmationModal` declares `accessibilityViewIsModal` and an `accessibilityRole="alert"` body, which is correct on iOS, but RN does not natively trap keyboard/TalkBack focus inside Modal on Android, and there is no explicit return-focus after dismissal. The destructive-delete flow is also a **single-confirm** today; WCAG 3.3.4 (Error Prevention) recommends a multi-step confirmation pattern for high-consequence irreversible actions — this is the only ❌ in the matrix and the only finding I'd consider Should-fix before public launch.

Three Should-fix items (Loading text-only state in `ResourceDetailScreen`, FlatList parent role, photo-picker change announcement) and five Nice-to-haves complete the list. Detail follows.
