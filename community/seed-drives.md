# Seed Drive Ideas

**Owner:** Casey (Community Manager).
**Status:** Day-0 placeholder.

A "seed drive" is a coordinated first-week-of-launch push to populate a city's marketplace with enough resources that the first arriving users actually find something useful. An empty marketplace kills retention faster than any bug.

## Ideas to evaluate (Casey ranks after talking to seed communities)

- _(Examples — Casey rewrites based on real partners:)_
- Partner with a local food bank's monthly surplus distribution; pre-list overflow on Mutual Mesh
- Coordinate with a tenant union to seed building-level give-aways
- Map donation drop-off events for the first month; "if you're dropping off there, also list 2 items here"
- Pediatric formula partnership (highest-trust use case; pediatric nurses can validate listings)

## What a successful seed drive looks like

- A new user opens the app, scrolls the feed, and sees ≥3 items they'd actually use within their first session
- ≥50% of items posted in week 1 get claimed within 72 hours
- No verification admin is overwhelmed (sustainable rate, not a launch surge)
- Zero privacy incidents

## What we don't do

- **No "fake" listings to make the marketplace look populated.** That's a trust-killer.
- **No volunteer guilt-tripping.** If a partner organization doesn't have the bandwidth, we wait — we don't squeeze them.
