# Accessing the Mutual Mesh Web Demo

**The web demo is live at `https://mutual-mesh.vercel.app`.**

It's the same app as mobile — the full resource feed, posting flow, and profile — accessible from any browser on any device. No app store required.

---

## You'll need an invite to get in

Mutual Mesh doesn't have guest mode. That's intentional: we don't show resources to people who aren't verified community members, because the people posting here are trusting the network with real information about what they need and what they have. Keeping that trust means keeping the door closed to strangers.

If you've been invited to the preview, you'll receive an invite code from the person who referred you. Sign up at the link above, paste your code, and wait for a verification admin in your community to approve your account (usually within 24 hours). After that, you're in.

If you don't have an invite yet and want one, reach out to whoever pointed you toward Mutual Mesh — they can generate a code for you.

---

## What you'll find inside

Once you're verified, the web demo works the same as the mobile app:

- Browse available resources in your area
- Post something you have to offer
- Claim something you need (your contact handle is shared with the poster — no public posts, no public replies)
- Manage your profile and, if you ever want out, delete your account in one step

Your data is the same across web and mobile. If you use both, you're looking at the same account.

---

## A note on privacy

We know many people come to Mutual Mesh because other platforms haven't been safe for them. The web demo runs the same privacy model as the mobile app: no real names, no GPS, no third-party trackers, and no public browsing. For the full picture of what we collect and why, see [`privacy-plain-language.md`](./privacy-plain-language.md).
