## Mutual Mesh — what we don't do

A short list of things partners sometimes expect from a "tech tool for mutual aid" that Mutual Mesh deliberately doesn't do. Read this before we start a pilot together — if any of these are dealbreakers, it's better to know now.

### We don't grow by going broad

We seed small, dense networks first. We don't run ad campaigns. We don't aim for a launch event. If you're hoping Mutual Mesh will bring a wave of new people to your network — it won't, and that's by design. A flood of strangers is the opposite of what makes mutual aid work.

### We don't accept paid acquisition

Nobody is paid to refer users. No referral rewards. No "invite 5 friends" mechanics. The invite mechanism is intentionally opaque — we don't even know who invited whom (invite codes are hashed).

### We don't share user data for grant reporting

If you partner with a funder who requires "anonymized usage reports" or "impact data" on the people using the app, we can't do that. We don't have the data to share. If your funding model requires this, we're the wrong tool — please use something else.

### We don't run press

No interviews about "the trans HRT sharing app". No TikToks. No mainstream coverage. The threat model for the people using Mutual Mesh makes virality dangerous. If an in-network journalist asks AND Sky approves AND Jordan (privacy) signs off, that's the exception. Default is no.

### We don't partner with for-profit retailers

No co-marketing with TooGoodToGo, no "powered by" deals with grocery chains, no embedded in food-bank-broker apps. Their data practices would contaminate ours.

### We will walk away if our presence isn't helping

Three weeks into a pilot, if exchanges per week are under five and the community isn't getting value, we wind it down. We don't double down with more outreach to try to "rescue the pilot". Mutual Mesh either helps your network or gets out of the way.

### 90-day "are we helping?" check-in

Ninety days after launch with your network, Sky writes you (one email, not a survey) and asks two questions:

1. Is Mutual Mesh helping your network?
2. Is there anything we're doing that's making things harder?

If the answer to #1 is "not really" or #2 is "yes", we pause and either fix it or wind it down gracefully. No metrics dashboards, no QBR. Just one human asking one other human.
