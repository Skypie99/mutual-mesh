# Intro / Listening Session — Universal First Contact

**Owner:** Casey. **Status:** DRAFT v1 — 2026-05-24. Sky reviews and approves before any send.

**When to use:** First contact with any Tier-1 partner network (community fridge, postpartum group, harm-reduction network, tenant union, refugee/newcomer org). Per `growth-strategy.md` step 1: "Talk first. No pitch."

**What this is NOT:** a pitch, a demo invite, a partnership ask, a "we'd love to support your work" feeler. The whole point is to listen.

---

## Subject line variants (pick one)

1. `Quick question about how your group coordinates`
2. `Listening, not pitching — 30 min if you have it`
3. `Asking about mutual-aid coordination (no ask attached)`

Avoid: anything with "partnership", "opportunity", "scaling", "launch", an emoji, or an exclamation mark.

---

## Body

> Hi {FIRST_NAME},
>
> I'm Sky. I'm working on a small, privacy-first tool called Mutual Mesh that's meant to help neighborhood networks coordinate sharing food and survival resources without a corporate platform in the middle. I'm reaching out because I'd like to learn how your group already coordinates today — what's working, where the friction is — before I assume anything is needed.
>
> Would you be open to a 30-minute call at your convenience? No slides, no demo, no commitment afterwards. I just want to listen and take notes. If anything I'm building turns out to be useful to you later, that's a separate conversation.
>
> If this isn't a fit, or it's the wrong moment, no need to reply — I won't follow up. I know inboxes are full.
>
> Thanks for the work you do.
>
> Sky
> skylerhalisky@gmail.com

## Footer (always include, single line)

> _No sales reply chain. If we don't hear back we don't follow up._

## Casey's notes on this template

- **Word count target:** body under 160 words. Current draft is around 150.
- **No "the team", no "we're a startup".** Mutual Mesh has one human voice externally — Sky's (per `mission.md`: "Only one human voice speaks for the project externally").
- **The out is real.** If they ghost, the partners.md status moves to "Closed — no response" and we move on. No nurture sequence. Per growth-strategy "What we don't do".
- **No personalization theatre.** `{FIRST_NAME}` is fine because it's literally their first name. Anything that pretends to know their work specifically ("I saw your amazing post about…") when Sky hasn't actually read their work is a marketing trick and we don't do those.
- **OK to add one genuine sentence of context** if Sky has actually read something they wrote or attended an event they ran. Otherwise leave the body as-is.
- **Don't attach anything.** No 1-pager, no deck, no link. If the listening session happens and they ask for more, then send `what-this-is-1pager.md`.
